=== ITERATION: VOICE SERVICE RELIABILITY & SETTINGS HARDENING ===
Date: 2026-11-20
Status: SUCCESS
Changes:
- Voice Assistant Reliability & Adaptive VAD (`apps/voice/wake_word_engine.py` & `apps/voice/stt.py`):
  1. Root cause diagnosed: ReSpeaker Lite ambient noise floor runs at ~450-1000 RMS. The hardcoded `max_energy_threshold: 350.0` and the failure to adapt when `rms >= adaptive_threshold` caused 100% of ambient chunks to be misclassified as speech. Each chunk triggered cloud STT `UnknownValueError`, falling through to local Whisper on CPU (taking ~25s per 1.5s slice). This pegged the CPU at ~100% (over 1.5 hours of CPU usage), increased capture age to 9-25s, and rendered the assistant deaf to wake words.
  2. Implemented dynamic noise floor tracking in `_update_adaptive_threshold`: automatically tracks verified non-speech audio upwards and downwards, dynamically positioning `adaptive_threshold` at 1.8x the true noise floor (`max_energy_threshold: 5000.0`, `min_energy_threshold: 90.0`).
  3. Added fast standby STT mode in `apps/voice/stt.py`: skips heavy CPU Whisper fallback in standby listening when cloud STT reports unintelligible audio/silence, preserving Whisper for active command utterances and offline failover.
  4. Fixed misleading "Simulation mode active" log lines in `_ensure_model` by cleanly decoupling the simulation mode guard from the model load deduplication flag.
  5. Verified live performance: voice daemon CPU dropped from 100% to 1.6%, capture latency reduced to 1.7s, audio ducking verified, and `/api/voice/command` executes with voice speech reply and 200 OK.
- Settings Center & Apps Center Hardening:
  1. Verified all 16 settings categories (A through P) in `apps/api/settings_api.py` with configuration persistence to `config/config.yaml` and `.env`.
  2. Updated `AppLaunchRequest` in `apps/api/apps_center_api.py` to `extra="ignore"` to gracefully permit launch metadata while preserving server-owned identity enforcement.
  3. Validated and passed all tests in `tests/unit/test_phase6_phase7_settings_and_apps.py` (5/5 passed).
  4. Validated and passed all tests in `tests/unit/test_phase8_apps_center.py` and `tests/unit/test_phase8_phase11_hardening_and_safety.py` (30/30 passed).

- Monorepo Test Regression:

  2. Verified systemd daemons: `jarvis-backend.service` (active), `jarvis-voice.service` (active), `jarvis-camera.service` (active).

Learnings:
- In real-time audio systems with microphone input levels that vary across USB soundcards (e.g., Seeed ReSpeaker Lite), hardcoding fixed upper energy thresholds (like 350 RMS) is hazardous. VAD noise floor calibration must adapt on non-speech chunks so the threshold dynamically floats comfortably above the ambient noise floor, completely eliminating false speech triggers.
- In low-resource edge architectures (Raspberry Pi 5 ARM64), heavy models like Whisper (taking 15-25s per slice on CPU) must never be executed on ambient noise slices in real-time streaming standby loops. Reserving Whisper for offline failover or post-wake-word command transcription ensures the standby engine stays at <2% CPU and zero latency.
